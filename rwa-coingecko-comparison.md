**Date:** February 27, 2026
**CoinGecko Category:** [Tokenized Products](https://www.coingecko.com/en/categories/tokenized-products) (~370 tokens)
**RWA.xyz:** [app.rwa.xyz](https://app.rwa.xyz/) (~2,000+ tracked assets)

---

## Summary

CoinGecko's "Tokenized Products" category contains ~370 tokens. RWA.xyz tracks ~2,000+ tokenized assets across 9 categories (Stablecoins, U.S. Treasuries, Non-U.S. Govt Debt, Corporate Bonds, Private Credit, Commodities, Institutional Funds, Stocks, Real Estate). Most major tokens overlap between the two platforms, but **CoinGecko lists ~27+ tokens/projects that RWA.xyz does not appear to track**.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Kinesis (KAU/KAG) is the biggest gap** - Kinesis Gold is a top-10 token in CoinGecko's tokenized products category with real volume, and RWA.xyz doesn't track it.

2. **RWA.xyz is more comprehensive overall** - With 2,000+ assets vs CoinGecko's ~370, RWA.xyz covers far more ground, especially in institutional products, private credit, and government debt.

3. **CoinGecko casts a wider definitional net** - CoinGecko includes experimental/crypto-native tokenized products (Unisocks, zBTC, MOONDOGE) that RWA.xyz wouldn't classify as RWAs.

4. **Wrapped token variants** - CoinGecko lists ~30 wrapped xStock tokens as separate entries; RWA.xyz only lists the underlying tokens.

5. **Real estate is a growth area** - Both platforms are expanding here, but neither has comprehensive coverage of DeFi-native real estate tokens.

6. **Niche commodities** - Uranium (XU3O8) and gemstones (EMRLD, VEREM) are on CoinGecko but absent from RWA.xyz.
