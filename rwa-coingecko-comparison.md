## Important Discovery: CoinGecko Has TWO Separate RWA Categories

CoinGecko maintains two distinct category pages for real-world assets:

| Category | Token Count | What It Contains |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| **Tokenized Products** | 578 | Actual tokenized stocks, ETFs, commodities, gold, T-bills, T-bonds, private credit |
| **Real World Assets (RWA)** | ~550 | Mix of actual tokenized RWAs + RWA protocol/infra tokens (LINK, XLM, ONDO, QNT, etc.) |

There is significant overlap between the two, plus CoinGecko's "RWA" category includes ~40 protocol/infrastructure tokens that aren't actual tokenized products.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Kinesis Gold (KAU) at $404M is the single largest gap** — A fully functional gold monetary system with real volume that RWA.xyz completely misses. Combined with Kinesis Silver ($16M), the Kinesis ecosystem represents **$420M in untracked tokenized precious metals**.

2. **Sygnum FIUSD at $47M is a notable institutional gap** — A Swiss bank (Sygnum) USD liquidity fund token. Institutional-grade product that fits RWA.xyz's mandate.

3. **UBS uMINT is tiny ($10.5K) but strategically significant** — UBS Asset Management's tokenized money market fund via DigiFT. Only 100 tokens in pilot phase, but it's a tier-1 bank tokenizing on Ethereum/Polygon.

4. **Pre-IPO tokens are an entirely new RWA category** — SpaceX, Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, and Anduril pre-IPO synthetics/SPVs exist on CoinGecko (~$15M total). Paimon SPV tokens represent actual fund shares. RWA.xyz doesn't track any pre-IPO tokenization.

5. **Tokenized AI compute (GAIB) at $39M is a novel RWA type** — GPU compute resources tokenized as yield-bearing assets. A new category neither platform fully addresses.

6. **Carbon credits ($44M via DOVU/CANA) are absent from RWA.xyz** — Natural RWA category that could be added.

7. **CoinGecko inflates its "RWA" category with protocol tokens** — ~40 tokens worth ~$18B (LINK, XLM, ONDO, QNT, ALGO, etc.) are protocol/infrastructure tokens, not actual tokenized RWAs. This makes the category look much larger than it actually is.

8. **RWA.xyz is far more comprehensive for actual tokenized products** — With 2,000+ tracked assets, deep private credit coverage ($18.9B), full stablecoin tracking ($297B), and institutional fund tracking ($3B+), RWA.xyz dwarfs CoinGecko's coverage of real tokenized products.

9. **Wrapped xStocks have $0 market cap** — The ~40 wrapped xStock tokens on CoinGecko are just listings with no circulating supply or trading. Not a real gap.
