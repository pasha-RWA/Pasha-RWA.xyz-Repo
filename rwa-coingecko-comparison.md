## Key Takeaways

1. **Kinesis (KAU/KAG) is the biggest single gap** — Kinesis Gold is a top-10 token in CoinGecko's tokenized category with significant real volume, and RWA.xyz completely misses the entire Kinesis ecosystem.

2. **Wrapped xStocks are the biggest category gap (~40 tokens)** — CoinGecko lists wrapped xStock tokens as separate entries; RWA.xyz only tracks the underlying 75 xStocks.

3. **Dinari has expanded beyond RWA.xyz's tracking (~30 extra tokens)** — CoinGecko tracks many Dinari tokens (especially crypto ETFs, niche stocks, and specialty ETFs) that RWA.xyz's 89 Dinari products don't cover.

4. **CoinGecko casts a wider definitional net** — CoinGecko includes experimental/crypto-native tokenized products (Unisocks, zBTC, MOONDOGE, Arianee) that RWA.xyz wouldn't classify as RWAs.

5. **RWA.xyz is far more comprehensive overall** — With 2,000+ assets vs CoinGecko's 578, RWA.xyz covers far more ground in institutional products, private credit ($18.9B), stablecoins ($297B), government debt, and corporate bonds.

6. **Niche commodities** — Uranium (XU3O8), emeralds (EMRLD/VEREM), and agricultural DeFi (AgriDex, Palm Economy) are on CoinGecko but absent from RWA.xyz.

7. **Crypto-backed ETF tokens** — The tokenized crypto ETF space (IBIT.d, FBTC.d, GBTC.d, ETHE.d) exists on CoinGecko via Dinari but not on RWA.xyz, likely because they're crypto-adjacent rather than traditional RWAs.
